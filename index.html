What each frequency changes

32 Hz: Deep sub-bass rumble.

64 Hz: Kick drum weight and bass punch.

125 Hz: Bass fullness and warmth.

250 Hz: Low-mid body; too much can sound muddy.

500 Hz: Instrument body; too much can sound boxy.

1 kHz: Core vocal and instrument tone.

2 kHz: Vocal intelligibility and forwardness.

4 kHz: Attack, definition and presence.

8 kHz: Brightness, cymbals and crisp detail.

16 kHz: Air and very high-frequency sparkle.
